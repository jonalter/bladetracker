    <h2>Why Track Your Blade Sharpenings?</h2>
    <p>Every ice hockey player knows that blade maintenance is critical to on-ice performance. But tracking when your skates were sharpened, what hollow was used, which shop did the work, and how your blades felt afterward? That's where BladeTracker comes in.</p>

    <h3>Built for Hockey Players Who Take Their Edge Seriously</h3>
    <p>Whether you're a competitive hockey player, beer league enthusiast, or skate parent managing multiple sets of steel, BladeTracker helps you:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Never Forget When You Sharpened</strong> — Log every sharpening with date, hollow depth, shop/tech name, and cost</li>
      <li><strong>Track Multiple Pairs of Blades</strong> — Manage tournament steel, practice blades, and backup pairs all in one place</li>
      <li><strong>Know When to Swap</strong> — See exactly when blades were installed or removed from your skates</li>
      <li><strong>Remember How They Felt</strong> — Add performance notes after skating to track bite, glide, and edge quality</li>
      <li><strong>Sync Across Devices</strong> — Your blade history stays in sync across iPhone and iPad with iCloud</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>Features Hockey Players Love</h2>

    <h3>Sharpening History That Sticks</h3>
    <p>Record every detail: hollow radius (5/8", 1/2", custom), who sharpened them, cost, and optional notes. See your complete sharpening timeline at a glance.</p>

    <h3>Blade Swap Tracking</h3>
    <p>Installing fresh steel for playoffs? Swapping back to practice blades? Log every install and removal with dates and optional notes about why you made the swap.</p>

    <h3>Performance Notes</h3>
    <p>Capture how your edges felt right after stepping off the ice: "Perfect bite", "Getting dull", "Lost edge mid-game". Build a history of what works.</p>

    <h3>Multiple Blade Management</h3>
    <p>Track as many pairs of steel as you own. Mark blades that need sharpening. Retire old blades while preserving their history.</p>

    <h3>iCloud Sync</h3>
    <p>Your sharpening log automatically syncs between iPhone and iPad. Update on one device, see it everywhere.</p>

    <h2>Who Uses BladeTracker?</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Competitive Hockey Players</strong> tracking tournament vs practice steel</li>
      <li><strong>Beer League Players</strong> who sharpen on a schedule, not a guess</li>
      <li><strong>Hockey Parents</strong> managing multiple kids' blade sets</li>
      <li><strong>Figure Skaters</strong> (yes, it works for figure skating blades too!)</li>
      <li><strong>Hockey Coaches</strong> tracking team equipment maintenance</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>Simple. Clean. Built for the Rink.</h2>
